Recognizes and translates text, offline too

Capture your screen, then do something with it.

Glimpse takes screenshots and screen recordings, lets you mark them up and pin them to the screen, reads the text in them and translates it. Offline when you want it, free and open source.

Features

Everything in one small toolbar

Capture exactly what you mean

Click a window or a single control, drag a rectangle, draw a freehand outline or take the whole screen. The screen freezes while you choose, so menus and tooltips stay put.

  • The mouse wheel goes from a window to the controls inside it
  • Adjust the area to the pixel before taking it
  • Scrolling capture stitches long pages (Windows)
Read in the guide →

Read the text, then translate it

Select any part of the screen and copy the text out of it, even from pictures and programs that do not let you select. Translate it in the same window.

  • PaddleOCR, Windows OCR or Tesseract
  • Edit the text before copying or translating it
  • Your own prompt for language models
Read in the guide →

A translated copy of the picture

Glimpse erases the original text, fills in the background and sets the translation in its place, in the original's color, size and weight, like a scanlation.

  • Lists, headings and alignment kept
  • Opens in the preview to save or copy
  • Works with every translation engine

Mark it up, then pass it on

Arrows, shapes, text, numbers, highlighting and blur; crop, cut out, resize or rotate. Then save, copy, or save and copy the file's path.

  • Numbers that count up by themselves
  • Blur or pixelate what should not be seen
  • Save and Copy Path, for tools that take a file

And more

Tools for every day

Screen whiteboard

Draw over the live screen while it keeps updating, over a frozen picture of it, or on a whiteboard. Pass-through lets the mouse work the programs below while the drawing stays.

Pin to the screen

Keep a captured area on top of every window, right where it was taken. Drag it, zoom it with the wheel, fade it with Ctrl and the wheel.

Screen recording

Record a window or an area to MP4, with the pointer and clicks highlighted and the keys you press, or a full keyboard, shown in the video.

Scrolling capture

Glimpse scrolls a page, a document or a list and stitches it into one tall image; or you scroll, and it stitches. Windows, experimental.

QR codes

Read QR codes and barcodes straight from the screen, no phone needed, and open the link.

Crosshair and color picker

A full-screen crosshair with coordinates: lock a point to measure the distance to another, or double-click a pixel to copy its color as HEX, RGB or HSL.

Screen ruler

A horizontal and a vertical ruler in a see-through cross, on top of everything, telling how far the pointer is from the cross, to the pixel.

Translation

Translation that works offline

Pick the engine that suits the computer and the text. Offline engines download their model once and never send your screen anywhere; online ones get the recognized text only.

Read in the guide →

Offline

  • Firefox translation Mozilla's Bergamot engine. Fast and light, runs well on office laptops.
  • Local language model Hy-MT2, Index-Translate or Qwen3 through llama.cpp, or any GGUF model. Uses the graphics card (Vulkan) and falls back to the CPU.

Online

  • Google Translate
  • Bilibili Index-Translate
  • DeepL
  • Google Cloud
  • Microsoft Translator
  • Claude
  • OpenAI-compatible (DeepSeek, Ollama...)

Build from source

Needs Qt 6.8 or later, CMake 3.24 and a C++20 compiler. Other libraries are found on the system or downloaded at pinned versions.

git clone %GITHUB_URL%.git
cd glimpse
cmake -S . -B build/release -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=<Qt dir>
cmake --build build/release --parallel
cmake --build build/release --target package
