Capture your screen, then do something with it.

Glimpse takes screenshots and screen recordings, lets you mark them up, reads the text in them and translates it. Offline when you want it, free and open source.

Download Source code

Windows · Linux (X11 and Wayland) · MIT License

640 × 360

Everything in one small toolbar

Capture

A window, a rectangle, a freehand shape or the whole screen, with an optional delay. Outside a freehand shape can be transparent.

Annotate

Arrows, shapes, text, numbering, highlighting and blur, then save, copy, or save and copy the file's path.

Record

Record to MP4 with the pointer and clicks highlighted, and the keys you press, or a full keyboard, shown at the bottom.

Recognize text

Copy the text out of any part of the screen with Windows OCR, Tesseract or PaddleOCR.

Translate

Select some text on screen and read it in your language, online or fully offline.

QR codes

Read QR codes and barcodes straight from the screen, no phone needed.

Color picker

Pick any pixel on screen and copy it as HEX, RGB or HSL.

Crosshair

A full-screen crosshair showing coordinates; lock a point and measure the distance to another, to the pixel.

Translation that works offline

Pick the engine that suits the machine and the text. Offline engines download their model once and never send your screen anywhere.

Offline

  • Firefox translation Mozilla's Bergamot engine. Fast and light, runs well on office laptops.
  • Local language model Hy-MT2 or Qwen3 through llama.cpp. Uses the GPU (Vulkan) and falls back to the CPU.

Online

  • Google Translate
  • DeepL
  • Google Cloud
  • Microsoft Translator
  • Claude

Build from source

Needs Qt 6.8 or later, CMake 3.24 and a C++20 compiler. Other libraries are found on the system or downloaded at pinned versions.

git clone %PROJECT_URL%.git
cd glimpse
cmake -S . -B build/release -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=<Qt dir>
cmake --build build/release --parallel
cmake --build build/release --target package
