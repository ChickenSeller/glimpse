# Scrolling Capture

Scrolling capture takes more than fits on the screen, such as a long web page, a chat history or a long list: Glimpse scrolls, captures as it goes and stitches the parts into one tall image.

::: warning Experimental
Scrolling capture is available on Windows only and still experimental. Some programs scroll in unusual ways, and the parts may not line up exactly.
:::

## Scrolling automatically

1. Click **Scrolling** on the toolbar, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>9</kbd>.
2. Choose the area to scroll:
   - Move the pointer over a scrollable control (a web page, a document, a list) and click; or
   - Drag a rectangle, then click inside it where to scroll.
3. Glimpse scrolls down and stitches; the height so far is shown above the area.
4. It stops by itself at the end of the content. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click **Stop** to stop earlier.

The tall image opens in the preview window, to save, copy or edit. See [Preview and Editor](./editor).

## Scrolling by hand

- Turn the mouse wheel while Glimpse scrolls to take over.
- Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> while choosing the area to scroll yourself from the start.

Scroll what you want through the area and Glimpse stitches it. If you scroll too far at once to line the parts up, it says "Not lined up: scroll back a little".

## Taking back the last part

If the last part stitched on is wrong, click **Undo** or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>, and scroll that part again.

## Preview

Beside the area, where there is room, Glimpse shows the image stitched so far, so that a misalignment is seen while it can still be fixed. In **Settings > General**, uncheck **Show a preview during scrolling capture** or change its opacity.

## Windows running as administrator

Windows does not let programs scroll a window that runs as administrator. For such a window Glimpse offers:

- **Restart as Administrator**: Glimpse restarts as administrator and can then scroll it;
- **Scroll Myself**: you turn the wheel, and Glimpse stitches as usual.
